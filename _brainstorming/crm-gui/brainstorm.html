CRM for Guest Houses & Small Hotels

Brainstorming session — 9 dimensions, 7 core MVP features identified

7 MVP Features 9 Dimensions Explored 1 Focus: Affordable Web CRM

🎯 The Problem

Small guest houses and hotels struggle with expensive, complex property management systems. Double-bookings, fragmented platform integration, and manual operational tasks drain revenue and staff time. Existing solutions cost $50-100+/month plus new hardware — prohibitive for small properties.

🔍 Nine Dimensions Brainstormed

  • Booking Integration: Double-bookings is the #1 frustration — it destroys trust and can represent 20-30% of daily revenue
  • Daily Operations: All tasks are tedious — housekeeping coordination, shift handovers, inventory management stand out
  • Guest Communication: Pre-arrival is the #1 touchpoint — sets expectations, reduces no-shows, starts guest relationship right
  • Rate & Inventory: Dynamic pricing is game-changing — small properties either underprice (lost revenue) or overprice (no bookings)
  • Guest Profiling: Unified profile (complete picture) enables personalization & repeat business
  • Reporting & Analytics: Full suite is the #1 decision-confidence builder
  • Team Coordination: Internal messaging should integrate not replace existing tools (WhatsApp, etc.)
  • Ease of Use/Affordability: Cost > complexity > new hardware are the #1-3 adoption barriers
  • Delivery Model: Web application — zero installation, works anywhere, automatic updates, responsive design

⚡ Seven MVP Features

Booking Sync Real-time multi-platform sync (Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, direct website) — prevents double-bookings, central calendar
Dynamic Pricing Simple rules engine (e.g., "increase 10% if occupancy > 70%", "decrease 20% if < 2 nights available")
Guest Profile Unified guest profile — one page per guest with basic info + preference history + stay history + communication notes
Pre-arrival Auto. Automated booking confirmation, check-in instructions, required docs, upsell opportunities
Commission Track Channel commission tracking — net rate after each platform's cut; total owed per month
Reporting Dshbd Basic dashboard — occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue by channel, growth trends
Task Lite Task coordination Lite — room status updates, housekeeping notifications, works on phones they already have

💡 Key Insight

The sweet spot: Combining double-booking prevention + dynamic pricing + unified guest profiles at <$50/month with zero new hardware creates immediate revenue impact and daily workflow reduction for properties that currently pay thousands for fragmented solutions.
