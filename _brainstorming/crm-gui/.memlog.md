(note) Brainstorming session across 9 dimensions: booking integration, daily operations, guest communication, rate/inventory, guest profiling, reporting, team coordination, ease of use/affordability, web application delivery

- (idea) Double-bookings is the #1 frustration - it destroys trust and can represent 20-30% of daily revenue for small properties
- (idea) All daily operational tasks are tedious - housekeeping coordination, shift handovers, inventory management stand out as particularly friction-heavy
- (idea) Pre-arrival communication is the #1 touchpoint - sets expectations, reduces no-shows, upsells, starts guest relationship right
- (idea) Dynamic pricing is game-changing - small properties either underprice (lost revenue) or overprice (no bookings); even basic automated rules transform revenue
- (idea) Unified guest profile (complete picture) enables personalization & repeat business - knowing guest preferences, history, special occasions creates "wow" moments
- (idea) Full reporting suite is the #1 decision-confidence builder - occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue by channel, commission analysis, growth metrics
- (idea) Internal messaging should integrate not replace existing tools (WhatsApp, etc.) - status visibility is key, not another messaging platform
- (idea) Cost > complexity > new hardware are the #1-3 adoption barriers - $50-100/month is 10-20% of monthly profit for small properties
- (idea) Web application delivery is critical - zero installation, works anywhere, automatic updates, lower IT overhead, responsive design for phones/tablets

(technique) Started brainstorming across 9 core dimensions covering all aspects of small hotel/guest house operations

(idea) Real-time multi-platform booking sync (Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, direct website) prevents double-bookings, central calendar

(idea) Simple dynamic pricing rules engine (e.g., "increase 10% if occupancy > 70%", "decrease 20% if < 2 nights available")

(idea) Unified guest profile (basic info + preference history + stay history + communication notes - one page per guest)

(idea) Pre-arrival automation (booking confirmation, check-in instructions, required docs, upsell opportunities sent automatically)

(idea) Channel commission tracking (net rate after each platform's cut; total owed per month)

(idea) Basic reporting dashboard (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue by channel, growth trends)

(idea) Task coordination Lite (room status updates, housekeeping notifications, minimal front-desk visibility - works on phones they already have)

(convergence) Narrowed to 7 MVP features solving the biggest pain points while being affordable and web-based

(insight) The biggest opportunity is combining double-booking prevention + dynamic pricing + unified guest profiles at <$50/month with zero new hardware - this creates immediate revenue impact and daily workflow reduction
