# Product Brief

**Title:** CRM Application for Guest Houses and Small Hotels  
**Status:** draft  
**Created:** 2026-11-03  
**Updated:** 2026-11-03  

## Topic
CRM application for guest houses and small hotels

## Goal
Identify key missing functionalities for an affordable, web-based CRM with booking platform integration

## Problem Statement
Small guest houses and hotels struggle with expensive, complex property management systems. Double-bookings, fragmented platform integration, and manual operational tasks drain revenue and staff time. Existing solutions cost $50-100+/month plus new hardware — prohibitive for small properties.

## Target Audience
Owners/managers of small guest houses and hotels who need affordable, web-based property management with booking platform integration

## Form Factor
Web application — zero installation, works anywhere, automatic updates, responsive design for phones/tablets

## Key Constraints
- Cost <$50/month
- No new hardware required
- Web-based delivery
- Must integrate with existing tools (WhatsApp, etc.)

## MVP Features (7 core features identified through brainstorming)

1. **Real-time multi-platform booking sync** — Prevents double-bookings, central calendar across Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, and direct website

2. **Simple dynamic pricing rules engine** — Automated rate adjustment based on occupancy rules (e.g., "increase 10% if occupancy > 70%", "decrease 20% if < 2 nights available")

3. **Unified guest profile** — One page per guest with basic info + preference history + stay history + communication notes — enables personalization and repeat business

4. **Pre-arrival automation** — Automated booking confirmation, check-in instructions, required documents, upsell opportunities sent automatically

5. **Channel commission tracking** — Net rate after each platform's cut; total owed per month

6. **Basic reporting dashboard** — Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue by channel, growth trends

7. **Task coordination Lite** — Room status updates, housekeeping notifications, works on phones they already have

## Key Insight
The sweet spot is combining double-booking prevention + dynamic pricing + unified guest profiles at <$50/month with zero new hardware — this creates immediate revenue impact and daily workflow reduction for properties that currently pay thousands for fragmented solutions.

## Success Metrics
- Double-booking prevention rate
- Dynamic pricing adoption and revenue impact
- Guest retention through unified profiles and personalization
- Operational efficiency via task coordination and pre-arrival automation
- Customer satisfaction and reduced no-shows
