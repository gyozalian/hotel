Questions

Do I need a channel manager too?

No. Casa Verde CRM connects to Booking.com, Airbnb and Expedia directly.

What happens when two bookings overlap?

The room closes on all channels immediately and you choose which booking to keep.

Can my housekeeper use it on her phone?

Yes. Staff get their own login and only see what their role allows.

Is guest ID data safe?

Documents are stored encrypted in the EU and deleted automatically after the legal period.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from Settings → Billing. You keep your data export.
