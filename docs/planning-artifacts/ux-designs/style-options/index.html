Four directions for the Casa Verde CRM

The same dashboard slice (alert, KPIs, 7-day calendar, today's arrivals) in four styles. Pick one, or mix: for example B's colors and fonts with A's density. Each is just a set of tokens, so the chosen one replaces tokens.css and the pages keep their structure.

ACalm neutral

Like Linear, Stripe Dashboard, Attio. The current default look of serious SaaS.

  • Feel: quiet, precise, fast. Off-white, hairline borders, no shadows, one green accent.
  • Good for: owners who live in the tool all day; dense calendars and tables.
  • Risk: can feel generic and a bit cold for a guest house.

BWarm boutique

Hospitality brand feel: serif headings (Fraunces), cream paper, olive sidebar, terracotta accent.

  • Feel: personal, like the guest house itself. Stands out from Booking.com-blue tools.
  • Good for: small independent owners; also carries over well to the landing page.
  • Risk: serif numbers and soft pills take more space; needs care in dense tables.

CDark ops

Like Vercel and Linear in dark mode. Dark-first is a 2026 dashboard trend; channel colors pop.

  • Feel: technical, "control room". Mono numbers, bright data on black.
  • Good for: night reception, data-heavy reports.
  • Risk: wrong mood for most small hotel owners; best offered as a dark mode of A, not the main style.

DFriendly bento

Like the Airbnb host app and Notion: borderless white tiles on a tinted background, big rounded corners.

  • Feel: approachable, consumer-app, mobile-first.
  • Good for: non-technical owners and staff on phones (housekeeping).
  • Risk: closest to what you already disliked in Material; less "pro".
